# Legal and support release gate

The public Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Refund & Cancellation Policy, and
Support page are product-specific working drafts. They are not a substitute for
advice from a qualified lawyer and must not be represented as professionally
approved until counsel signs off.

## Required before paid or general-public release

- Have Indian technology/privacy and consumer counsel review all four pages.
- Insert the operator's exact legal name, entity type, registered address, and
  applicable registration/GST information wherever counsel requires it.
- Appoint and publish the legally required grievance/contact person, postal
  address, and response process. `support@explainora.dev` is the temporary
  single point of contact in the product.
- Create or forward `support@explainora.dev`, send a message from an unrelated
  provider, reply to it, and confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC and spam delivery.
- Confirm whether users under 18 will remain prohibited. If minors will be
  served, implement verifiable parental consent and child-specific safeguards
  before changing the eligibility clause.
- Reconcile the final refund wording with the actual payment provider,
  subscription interval, taxes, invoices, proration, chargeback process, and
  cancellation UI before billing is enabled.
- Obtain and review data-processing terms for Supabase, Oracle Cloud,
  Cloudflare, every enabled LLM provider, transactional email, monitoring, and
  the payment provider. Confirm cross-border processing disclosures.
- Verify production retention jobs match the published periods, including the
  365-day account-security audit limit and backup expiry.
- Record counsel, version, approval date, and next review date below.

model, users, launch dates, and place of establishment.
